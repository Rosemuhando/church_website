About Our Church

Full Gospel Churches of Kenya - Njiru is a vibrant Christian community committed to worshiping Jesus, teaching the Bible, and serving others with love. We believe every person is invited to know God and discover their purpose in Christ.

Our church is shaped by prayer, discipleship, generosity, and a deep desire to see lives transformed by the power of the gospel. Whether you are new to faith or have walked with Christ for years, there is a place for you here.

Our Mission

To help people know Jesus, grow in faith, and share His love with the world.

Our Vision

To build a church community marked by worship, healing, unity, and obedience.

Our Values

Faith, grace, compassion, accountability, and service.
