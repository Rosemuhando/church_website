WHO WE ARE

About Our Church

Full Gospel Churches of Kenya - Njiru is a Christian community committed to spreading the Word of God and creating a place where everyone can come and encounter God's love.

We believe that the church is more than a building. It is a family of believers who worship together, encourage one another and serve God and the community.

Our desire is to help people grow spiritually, discover their purpose and develop a deeper relationship with Jesus Christ.

House of Bread for All Nations

A welcoming place for people from every background to receive God's Word, find hope and grow in faith.
