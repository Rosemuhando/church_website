Give Through M-Pesa

You can send your tithe or offering through the church's M-Pesa Paybill.

Paybill Number 247247
Account Number 725807
Account Name Full Gospel Churches of Kenya - Njiru

How to Give

  1. Open M-Pesa on your phone.
  2. Select Lipa na M-Pesa.
  3. Select Paybill.
  4. Enter the church Paybill number: 247247.
  5. Enter the account number: 725807.
  6. Enter the amount you wish to give.
  7. Confirm the church name before completing the transaction.

Before confirming your payment, make sure the displayed account name is Full Gospel Churches of Kenya - Njiru.

Ways You Can Give

Tithe

Offering

Give freely and cheerfully as you support the work and ministry of the church.

Special Giving

Support special church projects, outreach and ministry activities.

Give Securely

Always verify the church name and payment details displayed by M-Pesa before confirming your transaction.
